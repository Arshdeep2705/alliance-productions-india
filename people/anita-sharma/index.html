Anita Sharma

Co-Producer & Music Director

Co-producer of Tipppsy, music director, and a live-stage performer with international touring credits — the music and production through-line on the slate.

Anita Sharma was Co-Producer on Tipppsy (2024) and now leads music direction and original music-video production under the Tijori Music label. She has directed and performed on releases including O Jaane Jaan and Kahun na Kahun.

Outside features she is a recognised live performer on the international Navratri circuit — a touring schedule that has built her a working network of stage, music-industry and festival relationships across India, the Gulf and North America.

Selected credits

  • Tipppsy — Co-producer · Playback singer
  • O Jaane Jaan — Playback singer (Music Video)
  • Kahun na Kahun — Director (Music Video)
