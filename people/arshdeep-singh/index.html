Arshdeep Singh

Executive Director · Alliance Media Pty Ltd

The Executive Director of Alliance Media Pty Ltd — the Australian subsidiary of Alliance Productions India LLP — and the strategic operator behind the studio's globally-positioned slate. Based in Melbourne.

Arshdeep Singh runs the international side of Alliance Productions India from end to end: production, packaging, financing and festival pipeline. He stepped into the industry as Production Manager on Tipppsy (2024), learning the craft from inside a full Bollywood production before taking the reins on something far bigger.

That something is Echoes of Us (2025). Co-produced by Alliance Media Pty Ltd alongside Pooja Batra's Glow Bell Inc., the short film collected 88 international festival laurels, swept the 11th Dadasaheb Phalke Awards (Best Director, Best Actor, Best Actress), and earned back-to-back screenings at the 79th Cannes Film Festival on 14 May 2026 and the French Riviera International Film Festival on 16 May 2026 — a launch trajectory more often associated with studios than first-time independent shorts.

From Melbourne, Arshdeep leads the company's international relationships — financiers, distributors, festival programmers and co-producers — with a deliberate mandate to make Australia and APAC a structural pillar of Alliance's slate, not just an export market for it.
