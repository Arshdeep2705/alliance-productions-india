Snehal Kulshreshtha

Director, PR & Global Outreach

Twenty years inside global luxury hospitality — now translating that relationship network into festival, financier and distributor access for Alliance.

Snehal Kulshreshtha leads PR and global outreach for Alliance Productions India. Her two decades inside luxury hospitality were spent building exactly the kind of senior international relationships — across Europe, the Gulf, Asia and North America — that festival programmers, distribution executives and private financiers move within.

At Alliance she runs press, partner introductions and festival strategy, and is a primary point of contact for international co-production conversations and investor briefings.

Areas of focus

  • Global outreach — International markets, partners and territories
  • Public relations — Press, festivals, brand communications
  • Marketing strategy — Positioning, audience development
  • Stakeholder engagement — Co-production and distribution relationships
