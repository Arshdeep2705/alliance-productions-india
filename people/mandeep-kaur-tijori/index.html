Mandeep Kaur Tijori

Director, Producer & Actor

Producer and on-screen lead with an international packaging mandate — the project lead on the cross-border slate.

Mandeep Kaur Tijori is a Director of Alliance Productions India LLP and the producing lead on the company's international slate, including Tom, Dick and Mary and Misfired: Bali 9. Her producing approach is built around performance-led material, culturally specific stories and projects structured from inception for cross-border financing and distribution.

On screen she most recently appeared as Jessy in Tipppsy (2024) and across the company's recent music-video work.
