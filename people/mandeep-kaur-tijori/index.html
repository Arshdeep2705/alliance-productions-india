Mandeep Kaur Tijori

Director · Producer · Actor

An emerging film producer and actor active in internationally positioned cinema.

As Director of Alliance Productions India LLP, Mandeep is involved in the development, packaging and strategic production of both Indian and global feature films. Her current slate includes a Hindi-language feature under Alliance Productions, alongside the development and international packaging of a biographical sports feature based on the life of a globally recognised footballer. The project is structured for cross-border collaboration and international market positioning, with formal announcements to follow.

Mandeep's producing approach emphasises performance-driven storytelling, culturally resonant narratives and projects designed for strategic market engagement.
