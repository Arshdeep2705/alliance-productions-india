The studio

Director-led. Festival-validated. Built for cross-border distribution.

Alliance Productions India is the production company of Deepak Tijori — recipient of the 2024 Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award and winner of 22 international and national awards (2025–26). The slate pairs commercial Hindi cinema with internationally packaged features designed for global theatrical and streaming partners.

Echoes of Us

A heartfelt story of love, loss and second chances. Directed by Joe Rajan, starring Deepak Tijori and Iulia Vantur. Eight 2025–26 international Best Actor wins for Deepak Tijori across SAIFF, Berlin, Barcelona, Austin, Hawaii, LA, Indie Short Fest and Independent Shorts — plus the 2024 Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award. Most recently, Tijori won Best Actor for Echoes of Us at the French Riviera Film Festival, Cannes (May 2026).
