The studio

Director-led. Festival-validated. Built for cross-border distribution.

Alliance Productions India is the production company of Deepak Tijori — recipient of the 2024 Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award and winner of 20 international and national awards (2025–26). The slate pairs commercial Hindi cinema with internationally packaged features designed for global theatrical and streaming partners.
