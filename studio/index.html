The studio

A studio with three decades of access and a slate built for the next ten years.

Most recently, Tijori won Best Actor (Founders Award) for Echoes of Us at the French Riviera Film Festival, Cannes (May 2026).

Our story

Director-led. Festival-validated. Built for cross-border distribution.

Deepak Tijori's on-screen work — Aashiqui, Jo Jeeta Wohi Sikander, Khiladi, Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa, Anjaam, Ghulam, Baadshah — placed him at the centre of the Mahesh Bhatt and Yash Johar generation that defined modern Hindi cinema. As a director, he has delivered seven releases including Oops!, Khamoshh, Fareb, Tom, Dick & Harry, Fox, Do Lafzon Ki Kahani and 2024's Tipppsy, distributed by Panorama Studios.

In 2025–26, Echoes of Us, a short featuring Tijori, won Best Actor at the South Asian International Film Festival, Berlin Shorts Award, Barcelona International Film Festival, Austin International Art Festival, Indie Short Fest, Independent Shorts Awards, LA Indies Fest and Hawaii International Film Awards — wins across four continents inside twelve months. Most recently, Tijori won Best Actor (Founders Award) for Echoes of Us at the French Riviera Film Festival, Cannes (May 2026). The 2024 Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award recognised his cumulative contribution as actor and director.

The current slate is built for cross-border execution: Misfired: Bali 9, drawn from the Australian true-crime case, is in pre-production for 2026–27; an untitled Hollywood feature is being packaged in parallel; and Tom, Dick and Mary extends the franchise. Those Australian ties are reinforced at industry level: Tijori serves as an Industry Advisor on the Industry Advisory Council of the West Australian Indian Film Council (WAIFC), the Perth-based body building film and television collaboration between Western Australia and India. The leadership team combines a working filmmaker (Tijori), a producer-actor (Mandeep Kaur Tijori), a global-relationships director from luxury hospitality (Snehal Kulshreshtha) and a music-and-production lead (Anita Sharma).
