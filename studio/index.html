Our story

Director-led. Festival-validated. Built for cross-border distribution.

Deepak Tijori's on-screen work — Aashiqui, Jo Jeeta Wohi Sikander, Khiladi, Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa, Anjaam, Ghulam, Baadshah — placed him at the centre of the Mahesh Bhatt and Yash Johar generation that defined modern Hindi cinema. As a director, he has delivered ten releases including Oops!, Khamoshh, Fareb, the Tom Dick and Harry franchise, Do Lafzon Ki Kahani and 2024's Tipppsy, distributed by Panorama Studios.

In 2025–26, Echoes of Us, a short featuring Tijori, won Best Actor at the South Asian International Film Festival, Berlin Shorts Award, Barcelona International Film Festival, Austin International Art Festival, Indie Short Fest, Independent Shorts Awards, LA Indies Fest and Hawaii International Film Awards — eight wins across four continents inside twelve months. The 2024 Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award recognised his cumulative contribution as actor and director.

The current slate is built for cross-border execution: Misfired: Bali 9, drawn from the Australian true-crime case, is in pre-production for 2025–26; an untitled Hollywood feature is being packaged in parallel; and Tom, Dick and Harry 3 extends a franchise with two prior theatrical releases. The leadership team combines a working filmmaker (Tijori), a producer-actor (Mandeep Kaur Tijori), a global-relationships director from luxury hospitality (Snehal Kulshreshtha) and a music-and-production lead (Anita Sharma).
