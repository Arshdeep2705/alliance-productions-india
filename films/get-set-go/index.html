Synopsis

“A wild adventure where one pedal stroke could change everything.”

Deepak Tijori leads a Gujarati action-thriller as Arun Gohil — a Robin Hood figure who, with his team of ‘Chiranjeevis’, takes on corruption through bicycle-borne heists in disguise and redistributes the spoils to those who need them. The trailer’s own line sums it up: “Not the hero you expected, the hero you needed.”

The film marks Tijori’s return to Gujarati cinema after nearly three decades — his last was Hu Tu Ane Ramtudi (1998). Directed by Arnav Kumar from a story, screenplay and dialogue by Jhinal Belani, it is a Valmiki Pictures production presented by Jaliyan Group, distributed by Rupam Entertainment.

Humanity before the film

Ahead of release, Team Get Set Go announced that the entire budget allocated to the film’s ground promotions is being redirected to relief for families affected by the floods in Gujarat — supporting affected households, helping farmers recover, and backing educational initiatives in the state. In Tijori’s words: “because for us, humanity is bigger than any film.”

An acting engagement for Deepak Tijori alongside his role as founder and Managing Director of Alliance Productions India LLP; Get Set Go is not an Alliance production.
