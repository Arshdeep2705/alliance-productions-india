About the film

The story

A bachelorette weekend on the Goa coast curdles into something unrecognisable. Tipppsy follows a tight circle of women whose ritual celebration — sun, sea, secrets — slips, hour by hour, into a tightening thriller. Director Deepak Tijori tracks the night with a patient, watchful camera, letting the music, the laughter and the warmth slowly thin into something colder.

It’s a film about friendship under pressure: how the people who know us best can also be the ones who corner us. Lit in the saturated blues and neons of a Goan night, Tipppsy is genre cinema with an emotional spine — a chase wrapped around the question of who, exactly, you can trust when the lights go out.

One weekend. Five friends. A night that refuses to end. — From the Alliance Productions slate
