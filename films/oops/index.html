About the film

The story

Deepak Tijori’s directorial debut is an unflinching, kinetic look at the city’s hunger for fame. Oops! follows Jahaan, a young dancer whose talent runs ahead of his patience, alongside his girlfriend Nikki and his closest friend Akash. The trio wants the same thing — recognition — but only Jahaan is willing to take any door, any deal, any compromise to get there first.

Equal parts comedy and cautionary tale, the film moves with the swagger of a music video and the bruised honesty of a coming-of-age drama. It’s a portrait of ambition in a city that promises everything and counts the cost later.

Some dreams you chase. Others chase you straight off the edge. — From the Alliance Productions slate
