Fareb

About the film

The story

Fareb opens inside a marriage that looks, from the outside, like a magazine spread — wealth, ambition, a fashion empire — and then a phone rings in the night and the picture cracks. Director Deepak Tijori follows Ria Singhania through the strange, slow-motion days that follow her husband’s possible murder, as she helps the police while quietly trying to recognise her own life.

Romantic, melancholic, and laced with suspicion, the film is a study in how grief and doubt can wear the same face. The thriller mechanics are precise; the emotional register is closer to a chamber drama. A noir lit in cashmere and rain.

The truth wore his face. So did the lie. — From the Alliance Productions slate
