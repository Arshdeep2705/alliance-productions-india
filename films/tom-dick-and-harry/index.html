About the film

The story

Deepak Tijori’s breakout comedy starts from a deceptively simple premise: a deaf man, a blind man, and a mute man — best friends — accidentally walk into the wrong story. What unfolds in Tom, Dick, and Harry is a screwball caper of mistaken identity, loose cash, and two women who upend everything. The handicaps aren’t a punchline; they’re the engine of the comedy, each friend solving the world the others can’t see, hear, or speak.

Generous, fast-footed and warm-hearted, the film became one of the studio’s signature properties — a comic universe Tijori has continued to revisit. Made for big audiences and big laughs, it earns both honestly.

Three friends. One impossible weekend. Trouble none of them saw coming. — From the Alliance Productions slate
