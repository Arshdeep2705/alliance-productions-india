About the film

The story

Fox opens on a man whose reputation has already collapsed once — a disgraced lawyer who reinvented himself as a novelist, only to find handcuffs waiting at the next chapter. Deepak Tijori directs and produces a procedural that treats its central question — did he do it — as the surface of something deeper. What does a writer of crime know that a reader doesn’t?

Mood-driven and methodical, the film leans into shadow and silence. It’s less a whodunit than a portrait of a man who may have written his own confession years before the killings began. A crime mystery for viewers who want their thrillers patient, literary, and unsettlingly close to the bone.

Every crime he wrote came true. Then they came for him. — From the Alliance Productions slate
