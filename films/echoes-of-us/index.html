  • Director : Joe Rajan
  • Producers : Pooja Batra · Alliance Media Pty Ltd
  • Cast : Deepak Tijori, Iulia Vantur, Alessandra
  • Format : Short film · English
  • Laurels : 15 Best Film · 20 Best Actor · 88+ international wins & selections
  • Recent : Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award 2024 · SAIFFF Florida 2026 (Sweep)

Synopsis

A heartfelt story of love, loss and second chances. Two strangers, bound by what is no longer there. Joe Rajan's chamber piece moves with a patient camera through the rooms of memory — letting absence speak louder than presence.

Cast & direction

A small ensemble, told in close-up.

Joe Rajan directs Deepak Tijori opposite Romanian-born actress Iulia Vantur in her debut performance — alongside Alessandra. Pooja Batra produces, in collaboration with Australia's Alliance Media Pty Ltd. The film was Tijori's first ever award nomination in a 35-year career, going on to win eight Best Actor prizes inside twelve months.
