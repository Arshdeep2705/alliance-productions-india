  • Director : Joe Rajan
  • Producers : Pooja Batra · Alliance Media Pty Ltd
  • Cast : Deepak Tijori, Iulia Vantur, Alessandra
  • Format : Short film · English
  • Laurels : 15 Best Film · 22 Best Actor · 88+ international wins & selections
  • Recent : Dadasaheb Phalke Foundation Award 2024 · SAIFFF Florida 2026 (Sweep)

Synopsis

A heartfelt story of love, loss and second chances. Two strangers, bound by what is no longer there. Joe Rajan's chamber piece moves with a patient camera through the rooms of memory — letting absence speak louder than presence.

Cast & direction

A small ensemble, told in close-up.

Joe Rajan directs Deepak Tijori opposite Iulia Vantur and Alessandra. Pooja Batra produces, in collaboration with Australia's Alliance Media Pty Ltd. Tijori's performance has carried the film to 22 Best Actor honours worldwide — capped by Best Actor at the French Riviera Film Festival in Cannes, May 2026.
